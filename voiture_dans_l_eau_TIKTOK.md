## Sources (method checked)
- The SWOC rule (Seatbelts off, Windows open, Out, Children first) and the roughly 60-second window come from Prof. Gordon Giesbrecht's research, as presented by NPR, Kids and Cars, and CBS.
- The door is not the way out: water pressure blocks it. Don't wait for the pressure to equalise.
