## Facts
- Small bodies fall more slowly: air drag per unit mass is higher (square–cube law), which is why small animals survive falls that kill large ones.
- The biologist J. B. S. Haldane (« On Being the Right Size », 1926): for an insect, a drink of water is as dangerous as leaning over a precipice for a human, because of surface tension.
