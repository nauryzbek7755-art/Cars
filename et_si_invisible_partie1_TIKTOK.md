## Fact
To see, the retina has to absorb light. A fully transparent eye lets it pass straight through, so a truly invisible person would be blind. This is a classic physics argument (see the Atlas Obscura piece « The Problem With Invisibility Is The Blindness »).

## Partie 2 (cliffhanger)
« Ce qui se passe quand tu manges » — food would stay visible in the stomach until it was digested (H. G. Wells' idea in « The Invisible Man », 1897).
