## How to generate the clips
1. Open a free AI video generator: **CapCut (AI video)**, **Kling**, **Hailuo**, **Meta AI** or similar.
2. In the settings pick **9:16 (vertical)**, length **5 s** (or the maximum available), **realistic / cinematic** style.
3. Paste each prompt below **as-is, in English** (generators understand English best).
4. Generate **all 10 scenes**. If the result is bad, press regenerate 1–2 times.
5. Send me all 10 clips, named 01…10 or in order. I'll put together the finished video.

The character description is the same in every prompt so he looks alike from scene to scene.
When publishing on TikTok, turn on the **« Contenu généré par l'IA »** label.
